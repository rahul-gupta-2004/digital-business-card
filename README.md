# Digital Business Card & Multi-Domain Linktree

A sleek, minimalist, mobile-first personal linktree and digital business card built with **Vanilla HTML5**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **JavaScript**. Designed with a clean shadcn/ui aesthetic, dark/light mode support, and an interactive share modal.

## Multi-Domain Subdomain Mapping
